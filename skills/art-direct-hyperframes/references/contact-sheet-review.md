# Contact-Sheet Review

Use a contact sheet to judge the sequence as a visual system rather than approving isolated attractive frames.

## Required Views

Review:

1. One hero frame from every scene at the intended timestamp
2. All hero frames together at thumbnail size
3. Important frames at full size
4. At least one complete draft playback

Record the file or URL used for each view in the review scorecard. Missing evidence is a blocker.

Use [review-capture-plan.md](review-capture-plan.md) and the bundled `prepare_visual_review.py` script to generate consistent evidence from a rendered draft. Manual captures remain acceptable when FFmpeg is unavailable, but record exact timestamps and preserve narrative order.

## Contact-Sheet Pass

At thumbnail size, verify:

- The main focal point is identifiable in every frame.
- The sequence has a visible arrival, development, contrast, climax, and resolve.
- Three consecutive scenes do not repeat the same spatial archetype unless repetition is intentional.
- Stable identity anchors connect the sequence.
- Crop, scale, density, depth, or image-to-type balance evolves at structural beats.
- The climax is visibly stronger, not merely busier.
- Text-heavy frames do not collapse into web-page or slide layouts.
- When titles, logos, captions, and presenter are ignored, the project-specific transformation remains recognizable.
- The sequence cannot be relabeled as an unrelated productivity, AI, editorial, or business video without redesigning its central visual mechanism.
- At least 60 percent of reviewed frames carry primary project identity.
- Real source evidence is visible in at least 40 percent of reviewed frames.
- No more than 20 percent of reviewed frames remain generic.

## Full-Size Pass

Inspect:

- Crop intent and accidental obstruction
- Type size, measure, line breaks, spacing, and contrast
- Image quality, evidence legibility, and source treatment
- Edge tension, negative space, and optical alignment
- Brand, rights, and provenance issues

## Playback Pass

Inspect:

- Whether entrances reveal information in narrative order
- Whether the viewer has enough time to understand important evidence
- Whether stillness is present where attention should settle
- Whether one transition language connects the video
- Whether motion supports the selected route

Do not repair a structural composition problem with more motion. Return to the scene plan when the contact sheet fails.
