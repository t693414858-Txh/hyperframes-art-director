# Compare Visual Premises

Direct a 35-second product education video with readable UI recordings, one customer quote, and before-and-after metrics. The manuscript must both teach the workflow and establish trust.

Use the art-direction Skill first and compare candidate directions before selecting one.

## Evaluation Focus

- At least two candidates differ in narrative behavior, composition, and material hierarchy
- Each candidate includes three representative hero-frame concepts
- The comparison addresses content fit, first-glance focus, distinctiveness, thumbnail readability, and material credibility
- A palette swap or dark-mode variation is not treated as a separate direction
- The selected direction is justified with visible evidence
- The rejected direction and its risk are recorded
