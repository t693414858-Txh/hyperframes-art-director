# Route-Specific Review Checks

Apply the general eight-dimension rubric, then add at least three checks from the selected primary route. Record each check, visible evidence, status, and corrective action in the review scorecard.

## Precision Product

- Product or UI evidence remains more important than decorative atmosphere.
- Optical alignment, material edges, and macro crops look intentional.
- Restraint creates confidence; empty space is not merely unused.
- The result does not imitate a recognizable launch-film identity.

## Knowledge Explainer

- Each frame teaches one reproducible step or concept.
- UI, diagrams, code, or examples remain readable long enough to understand.
- Annotations point to real evidence and reveal cause before consequence.
- Instruction outranks product mystique.

## Editorial Documentary

- Images, captions, quotations, or archival evidence carry cultural meaning.
- Typography creates editorial hierarchy without becoming a fake publication page.
- Stillness and uneven pacing give important material appropriate weight.
- Provenance and context remain visible where credibility requires them.

## Evidence-Led Intelligence

- Every important claim maps to a readable source, comparison, or metric.
- Numeric hierarchy and semantic color remain unambiguous.
- The visual system supports a decision rather than decorating a dashboard.
- Result frames hold long enough for verification.

## Scientific Clarity

- Scale, causality, notation, and labels are explicit.
- Stylization never implies unsupported scientific evidence.
- Layering reveals complexity progressively.
- Warnings and uncertainty remain legible.

## Civic Trust

- Instructions and calls to action are immediately accessible.
- People and places feel authentic and representative.
- Claims, disclaimers, and official evidence remain readable.
- Hype, luxury, and opacity do not weaken legitimacy.

## Human Warmth

- Human moments and tactile details feel observed rather than staged.
- Source-derived color and camera distance support intimacy.
- Typography remains conversational without becoming casual noise.
- Synthetic polish does not erase lived texture.

## Craft and Heritage

- Hands, tools, place, process, and material evidence remain specific.
- Texture is authentic rather than manufactured antiquity.
- Cultural symbols are contextualized, not decorative.
- Rhythm gives process and continuity appropriate weight.

## Kinetic Culture

- Energy changes across the sequence instead of remaining maximal.
- Event facts survive expressive typography and fast motion.
- Visual references come from the subject's culture, not unrelated trends.
- Strategic still frames make the energetic beats stronger.

## Brand Manifesto

- One central claim governs the sequence.
- Supporting images prove the claim rather than forming a generic montage.
- Escalation earns one clear climax.
- The ending resolves to a memorable, credible statement.
