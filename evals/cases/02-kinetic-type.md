# Typography-Led Announcement

Direct a 15-second horizontal announcement video for an open-source developer conference. No footage or photography is available. The only assets are the event name, date, venue, three speaker names, and a geometric logo. The tone should be urgent, collective, and slightly raw.

Use the art-direction Skill first, then describe the handoff to HyperFrames.

## Evaluation Focus

- The Skill does not force an imagery-heavy ratio
- Typography is treated as the primary visual material
- Information hierarchy remains clear at high energy
- Motion intent does not override HyperFrames implementation constraints
