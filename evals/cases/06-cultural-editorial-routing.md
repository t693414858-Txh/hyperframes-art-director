# Cultural Editorial Routing

Create the direction for a 50-second horizontal video about the restoration of a century-old neighborhood theater. Assets include archival photographs, present-day documentary footage, interview quotations, dates, and a map. The user asks for a New York Times-like cultural feature.

Use the art-direction Skill first, then describe the handoff to HyperFrames.

## Evaluation Focus

- The Skill selects `Editorial Documentary` or `Craft and Heritage` based on the narrative emphasis
- Archival and documentary evidence lead the design
- The named reference becomes editorial rigor, caption logic, and literary contrast rather than copied branding
- The route avoids fake newspaper pages and indiscriminate sepia
- Uneven pacing gives important historical images room to hold
