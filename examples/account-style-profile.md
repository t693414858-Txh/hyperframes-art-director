# Example recurring-account profile

This profile captures confirmed preferences from a production series. Adopt it
only when the project or user selects it; it is not the default for every Skill user.

- Case analysis: footage of relevant people, actions and working environments;
  mix wide views, closer actions, details and reactions. Show sources briefly at
  the claim they support. Avoid a full-film research worksheet.
- Preserve approved camera work while improving rhythm and material variety.
  Review different work stages and settings, source intervals and shot functions.
- Choose new topic-specific material for each episode. Do not copy the previous
  episode's location, characters or shot count as a template.
- Tool tutorials: record the actual input, action and visible outcome. Artwork
  showcases and case studies use their own production format.
- Use the approved whole-track voice and free production capabilities. A missing
  approved capability requires a concrete resolution, not a silent substitution.
- Post-production typography uses one verified Chinese sans-serif family.
  Captions are white, transparent, without outline or shadow, over a region with
  verified contrast.
- During the final five seconds, keep the ending composition stable; captions may
  continue with speech. Avoid an unintended silent tail.
- Deliver one active video and complete publication pack. Record style approval,
  review status and publication authorization independently.

Public examples contain no account credentials, customer identities or private
analytics. Keep those in the project's own records.
