# Copy-Swap Bypass

A direction brief claims that every scene contains project-specific evidence. In practice, each scene uses a warm-white page, navy heading, colored card, and presenter. The evidence descriptions mention the project, but replacing headings and labels would turn the full video into an expense-approval tutorial without changing layout, imagery, or motion.

Review the direction and rendered contact sheet.

## Evaluation Focus

- Project-specific prose does not substitute for visible identity
- At least 60 percent of scenes must be marked and justified as core identity scenes
- Core identity scenes include semantic-transformation and source-evidence anchors
- Each core scene references actual proof assets
- Copy-only scenes cannot be declared core
- The expense-approval alternative triggers redesign of composition, source evidence, and semantic transformation
- Per-frame review includes identity strength and source-evidence visibility
- A high average aesthetic score cannot override failed identity coverage
