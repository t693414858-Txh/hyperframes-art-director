# Imagery-Led Brand Story

Create the visual direction for a 35-second vertical video about a craft coffee roaster that buys directly from small farms. Available assets are six documentary photographs, one macro video of roasting beans, a logo, and a spoken script. The intended feeling is intimate, trustworthy, and tactile. Avoid polished luxury advertising.

Use the art-direction Skill first, then describe the handoff to HyperFrames.

## Evaluation Focus

- Photography remains the primary material
- Crop, texture, and typography choices feel documentary rather than luxury
- Scene durations follow emotional importance
- The output includes a concrete hero-frame plan
