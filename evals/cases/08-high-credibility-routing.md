# High-Credibility Public Information

Direct a 35-second vertical public-service video explaining a city's new heatwave shelter program. The manuscript contains eligibility details, opening hours, a map, a hotline, and brief footage of residents and staff. The communication must be calm, accessible, and trustworthy.

Use the art-direction Skill first, then describe the handoff to HyperFrames.

## Evaluation Focus

- The Skill selects `Civic Trust` rather than a dramatic campaign or technology route
- Essential evidence and calls to action remain legible
- The palette is light, accessible, and restrained
- Motion supports comprehension and does not delay critical information
- Credibility and sensitivity requirements are explicit in the diagnosis
