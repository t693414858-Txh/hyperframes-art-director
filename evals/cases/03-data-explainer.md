# Data and UI Explainer

Direct a 45-second vertical video explaining how an operations dashboard reduced incident response time from 47 minutes to 12 minutes. Assets include dashboard screenshots, a simple line chart, three metrics, and narration. The audience is operations leaders, and the result should feel credible rather than futuristic.

Use the art-direction Skill first, then describe the handoff to HyperFrames.

## Evaluation Focus

- Data and UI are treated as primary source material
- The palette and typography avoid generic AI styling
- The storyboard builds toward the 47-to-12-minute reveal
- The climax receives stronger scale and a longer hold
